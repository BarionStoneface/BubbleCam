Drag on the picture to draw a box. Watch boxes are where the pleco is tracked (around his cave, his favourite spots). Home is one tight box over the cave mouth, for telling whether he's in or out. Ignore boxes are cut out completely: bubbles, filter outflow, swaying plants.
